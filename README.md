Developed an interactive Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard to analyze retail performance, customer purchasing habits, and multi-location business operations. 

Key Metrics & Insights Delivered:
• High-Level KPIs: Tracked over $698K in total sales and a total footfall of 149,116 customers, uncovering average customer habits such as an Avg Bill/Person of $4.69 and an Avg Order/Person of 1.44.
• Operational & Hourly Trends: Visualized transaction volumes by the hour, identifying peak morning rushes between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM to assist with staffing optimization.
• Product & Sizing Preferences: Analyzed categorical sales distributions showing top performing products (led by Barista Espresso at $91.4K) alongside consumer size preferences (31% Regular vs 30% Large).
• Multi-Location Benchmarking: Compared performance across store locations, highlighting top footfall hubs in Hell's Kitchen and Astoria compared to Lower Manhattan.
• User Interactivity: Embedded responsive slicers for dynamic filtering by Month and Day of the week for granular data drilling.
